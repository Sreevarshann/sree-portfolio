# Technology logos

Used in the "Tools & Technologies" section to name tools. Brand marks are
trademarks of their owners; they are reproduced unmodified (colours aside,
where a brand's own single-colour or reverse version is used for dark
backgrounds) and not redrawn.

| [Devicon](https://devicon.dev) 2.x | MIT | `sqlserver.svg`, `matplotlib.svg` |

dbt, Power BI, Tableau, SQL and RAG have no licensed mark in these sets and
are shown as typographic tiles instead of approximated logos.

Regenerate with `node scripts/build-tech-icons.mjs <node_modules>` (see the
script header).
